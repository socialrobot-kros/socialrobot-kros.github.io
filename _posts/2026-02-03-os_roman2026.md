We are excited to announce our Special Session on **Social Human-Robot Interaction of Human-care Service Robots** at IEEE RO-MAN 2026!

The conference will be held in **Kitakyushu, Fukuoka, Japan** from **August 24-28, 2026**.

This special session focuses on exploring how social robots can interact with humans socially and facilitate the integration of service robots into everyday life. We welcome papers on topics including social perception, behavior recognition, emotion recognition, dialogue-based interaction, and applications in healthcare, education, and more.

This special session is supported by the Korea Robotics Society (KROS).
